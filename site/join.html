For-credit research

Psychology majors and students in related fields at the University of Oregon may earn course credit through research in the lab. Prior coursework in cognitive neuroscience or biopsychology is recommended but not required. Interested students should contact their advisor and email the lab describing their level of commitment and relevant experience.

Open

Volunteering

The lab also occasionally provides opportunities for unpaid volunteers. If you are interested, please email us describing your preferred arrangement and what you hope to gain from the experience.
